# Creative DNA · initial preferences

Source: Mark's supplied project brief and reviewed TINKERBELL taste direction on 2026-10-15. These are starting preferences, not universal rules or proof of an approved edit.

- Cinematic storytelling, dynamic pacing, strong engagement, intentional composition, and clear visual hierarchy.
- Use creative transitions, mixed media, typography, graphics, and sound design when they serve the story.
- Prefer original, modern, visually engaging work over generic templates.
- For motion, kinetic editorial collage and tactile treatment are candidates for short concept or teaser work; keep speech and information clear when those treatments compete with comprehension.
- Keep color, typography, darkness, and effects specific to the client and project. The warm-white app design preference is an interface preference, not a blanket video grade.

Choose a direction by recording goal, audience, channel, brand, assets, rights, budget, base direction, optional treatment, execution rules, reference takeaways, and a representative sample check. An inaccessible reference remains unreviewed. Do not infer audio or motion quality from a still image.

Precedence: approved project decision > client direction > this file. Changes to this file require Mark's explicit approval and an entry in `memory/approved-learnings.md`.
